## Brand & Style
The design system articulates high-end contemporary Algerian gastronomy. It discards antiquated colonial or rustic clichés, projecting instead the architectural confidence, coastal breeze, and geometric grace of modern Algiers, Constantine, and Oran. The voice is welcoming yet impeccably refined: hospitable, culinary-focused, and luminous.

Visually, the style synthesizes **Contemporary Warm Minimalism** with tactile Maghrebi nuances. It relies on pristine generous whitespace, soft ivory canvas backdrops reminiscent of bleached Mediterranean tadelakt, architectural proportions, and delicate tonal borders. The interface avoids loud ornamental clutter; cultural identity is expressed through meticulous attention to spatial flow, warm golden spices, rich coastal botanicals, and delicate circular forms reflecting the restaurant's namesake ("La Dentelle" / delicate openwork).

## Colors
The palette balances deep Mediterranean botanicals with sun-warmed North African mineral tones:

- **Primary (`#135D54`)**: A stately, deep Algerian emerald teal drawn from coastal pine, cedar groves, and the iconic circular crest. It anchors primary interactive states, premium packaging tags, and primary CTAs.
- **Secondary (`#C29B38`)**: Warm artisan ochre gold, referencing Constantinian brasswork, raw honey, and toasted cumin. Applied sparingly for fine status indicators, curated pairings, and subtle decorative accents.
- **Tertiary (`#D87442`)**: Terracotta saffron, used strictly for dynamic culinary badges, seasonal chef highlights, and reservation urgency.
- **Neutral Palette (`#1C2422`)**: Deep charcoal cedar for absolute typographical clarity without harshness. 
- **Surfaces & Canvases**: The foundational canvas rests on warm sand-ivory (`#FBF9F5`), stepping up to pristine white porcelain (`#FFFFFF`) on elevated cards, and soft pebble (`#F2EDE4`) for interactive wells and secondary container modules.

## Typography
To break away from traditional Euro-centric serif fine-dining tropes, typography is unified exclusively under **Plus Jakarta Sans**. Its sculpted geometry, wide aperture, and clean humanist counters deliver an ultra-sleek, progressive culinary presentation while maintaining organic warmth.

- **Headlines**: Tight negative letter spacing coupled with medium-to-bold weights yields high editorial presence across dish titles, menu tasting sequences, and wine pairings.
- **Body**: Set comfortably with generous line height to invite relaxed reading of culinary provenance, heritage ingredients, and tasting notes.
- **Labels & Tags**: Uppercase styling with expanded tracking (`0.04em` to `0.08em`) is reserved exclusively for dietary attributes (e.g., *TERROIR D'ORAN*, *ORGANIC*, *CHEF'S SIGNATURE*) and currency designations.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout employs a responsive 12-column grid on desktop screens, an 8-column grid on tablet devices, and a 4-column fluid structure on mobile:

- **Desktop (>= 1200px)**: 12 columns, max-width of 1280px with centered fluid framing, `gutter-lg` (2rem), and `margin-lg` (4rem) to create an airy gallery feeling.
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px)**: 8 columns, `gutter` (1.5rem), and `margin-md` (2.5rem) providing effortless menu scrolling and split cart review views.
- **Mobile (< 768px)**: 4 columns, `gutter-sm` (1rem), and `margin` (1.5rem). The checkout flow and tasting menu selectors collapse seamlessly into full-width rhythmic stacks.

Component spacing adheres strictly to multiples of 4px/8px, prioritizing vertical breathing space around high-resolution food photography.

## Elevation & Depth
Depth is articulated through gentle, ambient tonal illumination rather than harsh artificial shadows, evoking daylight filtering through sculpted arches:

- **Base Layer (Canvas)**: Background ivory (`#FBF9F5`) holds structural page foundations.
- **Surface Level 1 (Dish Cards & Content Blocks)**: Pure white porcelain (`#FFFFFF`) with a micro-thin 1px border (`rgba(19, 93, 84, 0.08)`) and an ultra-soft ambient shadow: `box-shadow: 0 4px 20px -2px rgba(28, 36, 34, 0.04)`.
- **Surface Level 2 (Floating Cart, Modals, Quick Ordering Drawers)**: Pure white with enhanced ambient dispersion: `box-shadow: 0 12px 32px -4px rgba(19, 93, 84, 0.08), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(28, 36, 34, 0.03)`.
- **Translucent Overlays (Sticky Navigation & Glass Trays)**: Frosted porcelain `rgba(251, 249, 245, 0.85)` supported by `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)` and bordered along the bottom edge with `1px solid rgba(194, 155, 56, 0.15)`.

## Shapes
The shape philosophy merges geometric modernism with the soft, organic circular curves of the restaurant's signature branding:

- **Base Elements**: Buttons, text fields, and standard list items use a smooth 0.5rem (8px) radius.
- **Containers & Feature Cards**: Key dish containers, menu section banners, and order summary modules adopt `rounded-xl` (1.5rem / 24px), providing an inviting, tactile framing.
- **Interactive Badges & Quick Filters**: Complete pill geometries (`rounded-full` / 9999px) to echo the circular brand medallion and maintain effortless gesture targets.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Add to Cart, Reserve Table)**: Emerald solid (`#135D54`) with pure white typography, `rounded-md` (8px), height 48px, inner padding `12px 24px`. On hover: transition to `#0E4740` with subtle elevation lift.
- **Secondary Action (View Menu Details, Pairings)**: Outlined 1.5px with deep emerald (`#135D54`), transparent backdrop, transitioning to soft pine tint (`rgba(19, 93, 84, 0.05)`) on hover.
- **Artisan / Accent Button (Chef's Tasting Edition)**: Ochre gold tint background (`#C29B38`) with dark cedar text (`#1C2422`), delivering high-visibility warmth.

### Chips & Filter Pills
- **Category Filter (Entrées, Plats, Vins de Mascara, Douceurs)**: Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), height 36px, `1px solid rgba(19, 93, 84, 0.15)` on sand background. When active: solid `#135D54` fill with white text.
- **Heritage / Dietary Tag**: Compact pill, height 24px, subtle ochre border (`rgba(194, 155, 56, 0.3)`), sand-gold fill (`rgba(194, 155, 56, 0.08)`), tracking `0.06em`.

### Product & Dish Cards
- **Menu Card**: Structured in white porcelain with `rounded-xl` (24px) corners. Features 16:10 ratio imagery with subtle scale transition (1.03x) on card hover. Price tag sits in bold primary teal alongside delicate gold provenance notations (e.g., *"Safran de Tizi Ouzou"*).
- **Sticky Order Drawer**: Pill-padded bottom dock on mobile, anchored by quick item counter, delivery/pickup switcher, and consolidated checkout CTA.

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Inputs & Selectors**: Clean 48px height, `rounded-md`, sand-ivory baseline fill (`#F7F4EE`), bordered by 1px neutral line (`rgba(28, 36, 34, 0.12)`). On focus: smooth transition to 1.5px `#135D54` stroke with zero jarring outer glow.
- **Quantity Selector**: Segmented pill component with minimal `-` and `+` touchpoints in emerald outline, framing a crisp numerical counter.
- **Checkboxes & Radios**: Custom emerald rounded square (checkbox) and concentric circle (radio), filling with `#135D54` and an ochre-gold check icon upon selection.
